title: The expedition
description: How the exploration mode begins - an artist you love, a home port, and the first contract.
---

The [exploration mode](/lyrid/concepts/two-modes/) starts dark. The expedition
is how it begins: you name an artist you love, and the sky starts from them.

## Naming an artist you love

Right after you choose the exploration mode, lyrid asks one question: which
artist do you love? Type a name and pick the star. Any artist on the sky can be
the answer, except MusicBrainz's special purpose artists - Various Artists,
`[unknown]` and the rest - which are credits, not acts.

That star becomes your **home port**, chosen once. Three things happen at once:

- **It is lit.** You love them, so you have heard them: the star opens as
  *named*, the one way a star opens without being played or followed here.
- **You land on it.** The camera flies there and its card opens, and when the
  artist has a channel that plays here, the player starts it.
- **Its echoes appear** - the dashed rings of its closest unheard stars, the
  [next steps](/lyrid/concepts/the-fog/#echoes) of the fog.

You can put the question off. The sky stays dark until you answer, and the
question comes back on your next visit, or from the button under the hint.

## The first contract

The expedition hands you a contract: **chart your home sector** - open three
new stars near your home port. The panel in the corner shows how far it has
come, three pips filling as you go, and two ways forward: to the next echo, and
home.

**The home sector** is your home port's neighbourhood by the similarity the
sky was laid out from - the same neighbours its echoes are drawn from. So
following the echoes is always the way to fulfil it. Being near on the map is
not the same thing: the closest similar star of a well-known artist sits about
85 units away, six times the reach of the fog's light.

**A new star** is one you open for the first time after the contract was
issued, in any of the ways that open a star: thirty seconds of sound, a link
followed out, or listening elsewhere that a linked account brings in. A star
you heard last year is not a discovery, however recently you heard it again.
If your history has already lit most of the home sector, the contract asks
only for what is left; if it lit all of it, the contract is fulfilled as it is
issued.

When the third new star opens, the panel says the home sector is charted, once,
and can be put away. The contract remembers the moment it was fulfilled - the
moment of that third opening - and stays fulfilled whatever a later canon does
to the neighbourhood.

## What is kept

Your home port, by MusicBrainz id, and each contract: what it asks, the star it
is about, when it was issued and when it was fulfilled. Progress itself is not
stored; it is your openings, counted. All of it follows a merge in a new
[edition of the canon](/lyrid/concepts/the-fog/#when-the-canon-changes) the way
your openings do, is in the [export](/lyrid/reference/accounts/) and goes with
the account when you delete it.

The home port is not the [home star](/lyrid/concepts/your-life-on-the-sky/):
that one is where your listening gathers and moves as you listen. The home
port is where you chose to set out from, and stays.
